About Us

We are first and foremost 2 best friends who fell in love. Our story is one that can’t be told in a few sentences, but is felt by everyone around us. Love to us is about coming home. A home that we built through consistent communication, trust, and long hugs. Getting to witness the full spectrum of the life that we share is truly indescribable. Every bad day will always have warmth at the end, and that is the most precious thing in the world.

We have been blessed to know each other since freshman year of high school and have been dating for almost 4 years. During that time, we have shared so much. We have gotten to go all up and down the East Coast, deep into the mountains of Kentucky, to Texas’s great expanse. Every experience a joy, doubled through hands intertwined. Every lazy Sunday after a wild weekend of laughter, friends, and family. Spent together in the greatest place in the world, with one another.

Everyone needs people; the bonds you share define you. We were blessed to find all the kinds of love a person needs in each other. Two sides of the same coin, forever intertwined by fate and choosing one another each and every time, no matter the stakes. We are so excited for the next step in our grand adventure, and thank you to every one of you who supported us along the way.
